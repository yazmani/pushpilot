Pushpilot is a fast, open-source Pushbullet client for Chrome, Brave and Edge. Send tabs, notes and files, see your Android notifications, and text from your browser.

Install free View the code

Unofficial. Not affiliated with Pushbullet. Works with your existing Pushbullet account.

The Pushbullet extension, rebuilt for today's browsers

The official extension was built on Manifest V2, which current Chrome, Brave and Edge no longer let you install. Pushpilot is a ground-up rewrite on Manifest V3 that brings back what people relied on, and makes sending the page you're on a one-click job.

Open it on any page. It's ready to send.

The page you're on appears as a card with its picture, title and site. Pick a device and press ⌘ Enter.

  • Watching a video? Start at 12:34 sends it at the exact minute.
  • Notes, links, files and images, by paste or drag and drop.
  • Send to one device, several, all of them, or a friend.
  • Right-click any page, link, image or selection to push it.

Links open themselves when they land

Desktop notifications with a choice of sounds, and links that open in a new tab as they arrive, like the original extension.

  • A feed of everything you've sent and received, with search and previews.
  • Choose what counts: pushes to this browser, to all devices, or to your others.
  • Each browser gets its own Pushbullet device, so pushes between them just work.

Your phone's notifications, on your desktop

See Android notifications as they happen, and handle them without picking up your phone.

  • Copy sign-in codes in one click, or automatically.
  • Reply to WhatsApp, Messages and other chat apps.
  • Run notification actions and dismiss them on the phone.

Text from your browser

Read your SMS conversations and reply from the keyboard you're already typing on.

  • Works with any Android phone running the Pushbullet app.
  • End-to-end encryption compatible with the Pushbullet apps.
  • Side panel that follows the tab you're on
  • Keyboard shortcuts for opening, pushing and the clipboard
  • Universal copy and paste with Pushbullet Pro
  • Snooze notifications for an hour or overnight
  • Light and dark themes
  • Your own sound, or one of four built in

Private by design

No servers, no analytics, no tracking. Pushpilot talks only to Pushbullet, and your history stays in your browser. The code is open for anyone to check.

Install

Chrome Web Store

Coming soon. One click to install in Chrome, Brave and Edge, with automatic updates.

From GitHub

Download pushpilot-*-chrome.zip from Releases, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.

Needs Chrome 127 or newer, or a browser based on it, and a Pushbullet account.

Questions

Is Pushpilot made by Pushbullet?

No. It's an independent, open-source project that uses Pushbullet's public API with your own account. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pushbullet.

A push didn't reach my phone.

Pushbullet keeps old device registrations around as "not connected"; pushes to them never arrive. Settings › Devices shows which ones are live. Opening the Pushbullet app on the phone reconnects it.

Do phone notifications and SMS work on iPhone?

No. Pushbullet supports notification mirroring and SMS on Android only.

Is there a limit?

Free Pushbullet accounts can send 500 pushes a month. Receiving, phone notifications and SMS aren't affected. Pushpilot itself is free.

Pushpilot is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and made by one person, with no ads. If it saves you time, you can help keep it going.
